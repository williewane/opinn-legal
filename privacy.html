1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Opinn ("we," "us," "our") collects when you use the Opinn app (as a retailer or a wholesaler), why we collect it, and how it's used and protected.

Opinn is currently operated by an individual founder, pending formal business registration in Ghana, and is intended for use within Ghana.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide directly, and information generated automatically as you use the app.

2.1 Information You Provide

If you're a retailerIf you're a wholesaler
Full name, phone number, email address, business name, location (entered manually as text, e.g. "Kaneshie, Accra" — we do not track your device's GPS location), profile photo (optional) Name, phone number, email address, store name and location (manually entered), profile photo (optional)

When you place an order, we also collect the products and quantities ordered, your chosen delivery address or pickup preference, your chosen payment method (cash or Mobile Money), and — if paying by Mobile Money — the transaction reference number you enter. We do not collect or store your Mobile Money PIN, card numbers, or any payment credentials — payment happens directly between you and the wholesaler, outside of Opinn.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Push notification token: a device identifier used to send you order updates and store alerts. You can disable specific notification types in the app's settings.
  • Order and account activity: your order history, which stores you're connected to, and basic usage of the app, so the app functions correctly (e.g. remembering your active store).

2.3 Camera and Photo Access

Opinn requests camera access for two specific purposes: scanning a wholesaler's QR code to connect to their store, and optionally taking or choosing a profile photo. We do not access your camera or photos for any other purpose, and images are only uploaded when you choose to take or select one.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To connect retailers with wholesalers and enable browsing and ordering
  • To send order-related notifications (new orders, status updates, store availability changes)
  • To allow a wholesaler and retailer to contact each other directly when needed (e.g. for an order issue)
  • To maintain and improve the app's functionality and reliability

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising.

4. Who Can See Your Information

Some information is necessarily visible to the other party in a transaction, since Opinn exists to connect you:

  • A wholesaler can see the name, phone number, location, and order history of retailers connected to their store.
  • A retailer can see the store name, location, and contact details of wholesalers they're connected to.

We do not share your information with any other third party, except where required by law, or with service providers who help us run the app (for example, our database and push notification providers), who only process data on our behalf.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database and backend provider. We take reasonable technical measures (such as access controls on who can read or modify data) to protect your information, but no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

If you delete your account, it is deactivated immediately and your data is retained for 30 days — during this period, logging back in cancels the deletion and restores your account as it was. After 30 days, your account and associated personal data are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

Order records connected to a wholesaler's store may be retained for the wholesaler's business records even after a retailer disconnects from that store, as reasonably necessary for order history and dispute resolution between the two parties.

7. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Ghana), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data (most of this you can update directly in your profile settings)
  • Request deletion of your account and data (see Section 6)
  • Ask questions about how your data is used

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Opinn is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify users through the app before the changes take effect.

10. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of Ghana, including the Data Protection Act, 2012.
