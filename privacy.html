1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Opinn ("we," "us," "our") collects when you use the Opinn Retail app or the Opinn Wholesale app, why we collect it, and how it's used and protected. Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

Opinn is currently operated by an individual founder in Ghana and is intended for use within Ghana.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide directly, and information generated automatically as you use the app.

2.1 Information You Provide

If you're a retailerIf you're a wholesaler
Full name, phone number, email address, business name, location (entered manually as text, e.g. "Kaneshie, Accra" — we do not track your device's GPS location), profile photo (optional) Name, phone number, email address, store name and location (manually entered), shop logo and profile photo (optional), product details and photos, delivery and pickup settings, and the Mobile Money details you choose to show retailers at checkout

If you are a wholesaler, we also keep subscription records: your plan, subscription dates, and the payment reference you send us on WhatsApp so we can confirm your payment.

When you place an order, we also collect the products and quantities ordered, your chosen delivery address or pickup preference, your chosen payment method (cash or Mobile Money), and, if paying by Mobile Money, the transaction reference number you enter. If a retailer reports a problem with an order, we also keep the reason selected. We do not collect or store your Mobile Money PIN, card numbers, or any payment credentials — payment happens directly between you and the wholesaler, outside of Opinn.

2.2 Customer Details Entered by Wholesalers

A wholesaler may add customers who do not use the app (for example a name, phone number, location, and optional business name or email) so the wholesaler can create orders for them. The wholesaler is responsible for having a proper reason to hold and use that information. Opinn stores it only to provide the service to that wholesaler.

2.3 Information Collected Automatically

  • Push notification token: a device identifier used to send you order updates and store alerts. Retailers can pause notifications in the Profile settings, and wholesalers can choose which notification types they receive.
  • Order and account activity: your order history, which stores you're connected to, and basic usage of the app, so the app functions correctly (e.g. remembering your active store).

2.4 Camera and Photo Access

Opinn requests camera access for two specific purposes: scanning a wholesaler's QR code to connect to their store, and optionally taking or choosing a profile photo. We do not access your camera or photos for any other purpose, and images are only uploaded when you choose to take or select one.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To connect retailers with wholesalers and enable browsing and ordering
  • To send order-related notifications (new orders, status updates, store availability changes)
  • To allow a wholesaler and retailer to contact each other directly when needed (e.g. for an order issue)
  • To manage wholesale subscriptions, trials, and renewals
  • To keep Opinn secure and prevent fraud and misuse
  • To maintain and improve the app's functionality and reliability

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising.

4. Who Can See Your Information

Some information is necessarily visible to the other party in a transaction, since Opinn exists to connect you:

  • A wholesaler can see the name, business name, phone number, location, profile photo, and order history of retailers connected to their store. A wholesaler cannot see a retailer's orders with other wholesalers, and a retailer cannot see other retailers' information.
  • A retailer can see the store name, location, logo, products, prices, delivery and pickup details, payment instructions, and contact number of wholesalers they're connected to.
  • Your push notification token is never visible to other users.

We do not share your information with any other third party, except where required by law, or with service providers who help us run the app (for example, our database and push notification providers), who only process data on our behalf. These providers are Supabase (database, login, file storage, and server functions) and Expo, Apple, and Google (delivery of push notifications).

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database and backend provider. Our Supabase project is hosted in the EU (Ireland), so your information is processed outside Ghana. We expect our providers to protect it to a standard comparable with this policy. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access rules so users can only reach data they are entitled to, but no system can be guaranteed 100% secure. Please keep your password private and tell us if you think your account has been accessed without your permission.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can request deletion of your account in the app, under Settings, then Account Management. The account is deactivated immediately, and logging back in within 30 days cancels the deletion and restores your account.

6.1 Retailers

After 30 days, your login, profile photo, and notifications are permanently deleted. Your name, phone number, and location on past orders and shop connections are replaced with "Deleted retailer". Order records (items, amounts, dates, and status) are kept in the wholesaler's records so that the wholesaler's business books stay complete.

6.2 Wholesalers

Please resolve outstanding orders before deleting your account. After 30 days, your login and shop data, including products, orders, customer records, and notifications, are permanently deleted, and retailers connected to your shop will no longer see their order history with it. Subscription and payment records are kept for accounting and dispute purposes.

6.3 Other Records

Orders that a wholesaler deletes are permanently removed after 30 days, and wholesaler notifications are removed after 30 days. We may keep some information longer where the law requires it or where needed to prevent fraud or resolve a dispute.

7. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Ghana), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data (most of this you can update directly in your profile settings)
  • Request deletion of your account and data (see Section 6)
  • Object to certain uses of your data
  • Ask questions about how your data is used

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible. You may also complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission.

8. Children's Privacy

Opinn is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify users through the app before the changes take effect.

10. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of Ghana, including the Data Protection Act, 2012.
