6.1 Pausing a Shop

A wholesaler may pause their shop. While a shop is paused, the Retail app displays the shop as "Shop paused" and retailers cannot place new orders through that shop. Orders submitted before the shop was paused may continue to be managed and completed.

6.2 Wholesale Subscription

The Wholesale app offers a 21-day free trial followed by a paid subscription of GHS 50 per month or GHS 500 per year. Prices and plan details are shown in the Subscription screen of the Wholesale app before you subscribe, and the price shown when you subscribe applies. To subscribe or renew, the wholesaler copies the payment number shown in the app, makes the payment, and sends the payment reference to Opinn on WhatsApp. The subscription starts or renews once Opinn has confirmed the payment.

6.3 Expiry or Non-Renewal

The wholesaler can still log in, view and manage existing orders, complete existing orders, and edit products and stock. Existing orders may continue to be completed after the subscription expires. Editing products or stock does not reactivate a paused shop; renewal is required before the wholesaler can receive new orders.
