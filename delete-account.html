Delete Your Opinn Account

Last updated: [Insert publication date]

You can delete your Opinn account yourself, inside the app, at any time. This page explains how, what happens to your data, and what to do if you cannot open the app.

1. Delete your account in the app

Retail app (retailers)

  1. Open the Opinn Retail app and log in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account Management.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Wholesale app (wholesalers)

  1. Open the Opinn Wholesale app and log in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account Management.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm. Please complete or cancel any open orders first.

2. Can't use the app?

Email us at opinnapp@gmail.com from the email address on your account. Please tell us whether you are a retailer or a wholesaler, and the name on the account. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act, so that nobody can delete an account that is not theirs.

3. What happens after you request deletion

  • Your account is made inactive straight away, and you are logged out.
  • You have 30 days to change your mind. Logging back in during this time cancels the deletion and restores your account.
  • After 30 days, the deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

4. What is deleted and what is kept

Retailers

  • Deleted: your login, profile photo, and notifications.
  • Anonymised: your name, phone number, and location on past orders and shop connections are replaced with "Deleted retailer".
  • Kept: the order records themselves (items, amounts, dates, status), because wholesalers need them for their business books. They no longer identify you.

Wholesalers

  • Deleted: your login and shop data, including products, orders, customer records, and notifications. Retailers connected to your shop will no longer see their order history with it.
  • Kept: subscription and payment records, for accounting and dispute purposes.

We may also keep some information longer where the law requires it or where it is needed to prevent fraud or resolve a dispute. See our Privacy Policy for details.
